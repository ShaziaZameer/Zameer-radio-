# Zameer Radio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Zameer Radio plays live radio streams and displays station information. The app does not require an account, run advertising or analytics, or send your selected photo, favourites, or sleep timer settings to us.

## Information stored on your device

If you choose a photo, the app resizes it and stores it locally in the app's WebView storage. Favourites and the sleep timer setting are also stored on your device. You can remove a photo in the app, clear app storage, or uninstall the app to remove these local settings. The app does not request access to your entire photo library; Android's file picker lets you choose a file.

## Internet connections

The app requests the station directory from Radio Browser API servers. When you play a station, your device connects directly to that station's stream server. Station artwork may load from a station's website. These independent services receive ordinary connection data, such as your IP address and request details, and may handle it under their own policies. Station availability and content are controlled by the respective stations. The app does not transmit your selected photo, favourites, or timer setting to these services.

## Permissions and security

The Android app requests Internet access. The file picker is used only when you choose a photo. Radio station streams and directory requests use HTTPS. The app does not include a developer-operated backend or collect payment information.

## Children and changes

The app is a general radio directory and is not designed specifically for children. Stations may carry content intended for different audiences. We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The updated date and text will appear here.

For privacy questions, open an issue in the [Zameer Radio repository](https://github.com/ShaziaZameer/Zameer-radio-/issues).
